What We Do

Our Services

We specialize in keeping your asphalt surfaces protected, smooth, and looking brand new.

Driveway Sealing

High-quality commercial-grade sealcoat applied to protect your asphalt from UV rays, water damage, oil spills, and freeze-thaw cycles. Extends pavement life by years.

  • Coal tar & asphalt-based sealers
  • Squeegee & spray application
  • Cures in 24–48 hours
  • 2-year warranty on materials
Most Popular

Asphalt Repair

From potholes to full-depth patches, we restore your asphalt surface to a safe and smooth condition using hot mix and cold patch materials suited to every season.

  • Pothole patching & levelling
  • Edge repairs & border restoration
  • Full-depth patching
  • Surface milling & overlay

Crack Filling

Prevent small cracks from becoming costly problems. We use hot-pour rubberized crack filler to seal and waterproof hairline and structural cracks before they spread.

  • Hot-pour rubberized sealant
  • Hairline to 1" wide cracks
  • Prevents water infiltration
  • Flexible in all temperatures
